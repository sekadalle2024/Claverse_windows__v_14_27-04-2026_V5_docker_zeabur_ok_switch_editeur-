================================================================================
  TECHNICAL NOTES
================================================================================

Provision Reversals Logic:
  1. Provisions are liabilities (credit accounts)
  2. Reversals reduce provisions (debit movements)
  3. But reversals are recorded in 791X (credit = product)
  4. We extract credit balance and invert sign for display

Account Structure:
  791X = Reprises de provisions (Products)
  691X = Dotations aux provisions (Charges)
  19X = Provisions (Liabilities)

Calculation Formula:
  Reprises N = |Solde Crédit N - Solde Débit N|
